About

I'm a data-focused problem solver with a background in mathematics, education, and technology. I enjoy analyzing information, finding inconsistencies, and improving processes — and payroll combines the parts of my career I've been best at: numbers, systems, accuracy, and helping people.

As a math teacher, I managed and reconciled performance data for 150+ students every quarter, maintained confidential records in strict compliance with state and district policy, and built structured workflows for verifying and auditing data before it went out the door. As a junior frontend developer at ICU Medical, I validated and reconciled large datasets, kept audit-ready documentation of every system change, and worked cross-functionally to catch discrepancies before they became problems.

Those are payroll habits, even though I didn't call them that at the time: confidentiality by default, a paper trail for every decision, and a refusal to let "close enough" numbers go out the door. I built the Cypress Manufacturing case study below to prove that the transition is real — not just a resume line, but a way of thinking I can already apply to gross-to-net calculations, discrepancy audits, and compliance questions.
